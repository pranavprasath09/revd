Is the FD's apex seal fear overblown?

Owen Pike · 9h ago · 61 replies

I have owned three FDs across eleven years and rebuilt exactly one engine, which is roughly the same hit rate as my friends running early turbo Porsches. Nobody calls those disposable.

My honest read is that the rotary's reputation is a cooling reputation wearing an apex seal costume. Every failure I have seen personally followed a heat event that the owner either did not notice or did not log. Fix the cooling and the engine is ordinary.
